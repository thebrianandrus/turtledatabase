Record Holders

Champions of the shell

01

Deepest Diver

The leatherback has been recorded diving more than 1,000 meters down — deeper than almost any other reptile, chasing jellyfish into the dark.

02

Oldest Living

Jonathan, a Seychelles giant tortoise on the island of St Helena, is believed to have hatched around 1832 — making him the oldest known living land animal.

03

Longest Journey

A satellite-tracked leatherback swam more than 20,000 km from Indonesia to the coast of Oregon — one of the longest migrations recorded for any vertebrate.

04

Largest Ever

Archelon, a Late Cretaceous sea turtle, reached around 4.6 meters long — roughly the length of a small car.

05

Rarest

The Yangtze giant softshell turtle is down to a tiny handful of known individuals, making it a contender for the rarest turtle on Earth.

06

Smallest

The speckled cape tortoise of South Africa grows to only about 10 cm — small enough to sit in the palm of your hand.
