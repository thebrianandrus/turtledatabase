Secrets of the Realm

Laws of the open sea

01

The Magnetic Map

Hatchlings sense the strength and angle of Earth's magnetic field, using it like a built-in GPS to stay inside warm currents and, years later, find their way back to the coast where they hatched.

02

Born by Temperature

In sea turtles, sand temperature decides sex: warmer nests produce mostly females, cooler nests mostly males. A warming climate is tipping the balance.

03

Salt Tears

Special glands beside the eyes pump out excess salt from seawater and food. Nesting females look like they're crying — they're just desalinating.
