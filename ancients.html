Secrets of the Realm

Laws of deep time

01

A Shell Made of Ribs

A turtle's shell isn't armor bolted on — it's built from widened ribs and backbone fused with bony plates. It's why a turtle can never leave its shell.

02

Belly First

Odontochelys, around 220 million years old, had a full belly plate but only a partial top shell — evidence the shell was assembled piece by piece.

03

Survivors of the Impact

When the asteroid ended the age of dinosaurs 66 million years ago, many turtle lineages made it through — and their descendants are still with us.
