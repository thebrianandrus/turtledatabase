Why This Exists

Turtles deserve an epic.

Turtles have been on this planet for more than 200 million years. They watched the dinosaurs rise and fall, crossed oceans before there were maps, and carried their homes on their backs through every climate the Earth could throw at them.

The Turtle Database exists to tell that story the way it deserves to be told — as an adventure. Every species gets a record: where it lives, what it eats, what makes it extraordinary, and how it's doing in a world that's changing faster than any it has known.

It's also a call to action. Turtles are among the most threatened animals alive, and the more people who fall for them, the better their odds.

Our Principles

The code of the shell

01

Wonder First

Facts stick when they're thrilling. We tell turtle stories with the scale and drama they deserve.

02

Accuracy Always

Sizes, ranges and statuses are checked against scientific sources, and conservation statuses follow the IUCN Red List.

03

Protect the Living

We never share exact locations of rare species, nests or populations — that information can help poachers.
