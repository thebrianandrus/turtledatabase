Before You Report

Watch, don't touch

Keep your distance and let the animal go about its business. Photos from a respectful distance are perfect.

Nesting sea turtle?

Stay back, stay dark — no flashlights or flash photography — and contact your local wildlife agency.

Injured or in danger?

Contact local wildlife authorities or a licensed rehabilitator right away. This form isn't monitored for emergencies.
