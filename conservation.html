The Last Stand

CONSERVATION

They survived the asteroid. Whether they survive us is still being decided.

The Situation

Among the most threatened animals on Earth.

By most assessments, more than half of the world's turtle and tortoise species are threatened with extinction — a higher share than birds or mammals. Six of the seven sea turtles are listed as threatened, and the seventh is simply too little-studied to assess.

Turtles evolved for a world where living long made up for breeding slowly. When adults are taken from the wild faster than they can be replaced, populations that took decades to build can vanish within a few years.

The Threats

What they're up against

01

The Wildlife Trade

Turtles and tortoises are smuggled by the thousands for pets, food and traditional medicine. Rare, beautiful shells make some species a prime target.

02

Bycatch

Sea turtles drown in fishing nets and on longlines. Turtle excluder devices in trawl nets have saved many, but not every fishery uses them.

03

Habitat Loss

Drained wetlands, dammed rivers, developed beaches and cleared forests leave turtles nowhere to feed, nest or hide.

04

Plastic & Pollution

Floating plastic bags look a lot like jellyfish. Ingested plastic and entanglement in debris injure and kill turtles at every life stage.

05

Hotter Sands

Because nest temperature sets sex in many turtles, warming beaches are producing ever more females — and rising seas swallow nesting beaches.

06

Roads & Predators

Turtles crossing roads to nest are killed by cars, while raccoons, foxes and other predators thriving near people dig up nests.

Join the Defense

What you can do

01

Never Release a Pet

Released pet turtles can spread disease and outcompete native species. If you can't keep one, contact a rescue.

02

Help Them Across

If it's safe, move a turtle crossing a road to the side it was heading toward — never back where it came from, and never to a 'better' spot.

03

Lights Out on the Beach

Hatchlings head for the brightest horizon. Keep beachfront lights off or shielded during nesting season so they find the sea.

04

Cut the Plastic

Skip balloons and single-use bags, and pick up trash near water. Every piece you remove is one less look-alike jellyfish.

05

Buy Nothing Turtle

Never buy tortoiseshell products, wild-caught turtles or turtle eggs. Demand drives the trade.

06

Report Sightings

Your observations help track populations. Log what you see in our sighting form and with local wildlife agencies.
