Secrets of the Realm

Laws of the dark water

01

Breathing Below

Some river turtles can pull oxygen from the water through the skin and the cloaca, letting them stay submerged for remarkable stretches — the Mary River turtle is a champion.

02

Ambush Artists

The alligator snapper lures fish with a wriggling tongue; the mata mata gapes and vacuums them in. Neither needs to chase anything.

03

Winter Under Ice

Painted turtles survive months beneath frozen ponds with almost no oxygen, slowing their bodies nearly to a stop until spring.
