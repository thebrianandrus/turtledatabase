Secrets of the Realm

Laws of the dry world

01

Engineers of the Earth

A gopher tortoise burrow can run several meters deep and shelters hundreds of other species from fire, heat and predators. Lose the tortoise, lose the community.

02

Island Giants

Cut off on remote islands with few predators, tortoises grew enormous. On the Galápagos, shell shape even changed by island — domes in lush highlands, saddles where food grows high.

03

The Long Game

Tortoises grow slowly and breed late, which helps them live extraordinarily long — and makes populations very slow to recover from poaching.
